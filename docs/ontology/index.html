Description

Classes and properties for engagements in nuclear new-build procurement and the roles that organizations take in them, and for the reports of those engagements: the reported engagement-event clusters, the article-level records they are built from, the organizations and references they point to, the coding activities and quality measurements behind them, and the role, occurrence, resolution and identification claims that record which activity claimed each coded role value, each coded occurrence status, each resolution of an actor reference and each identification of a cluster with one engagement.

Definite Organization Reference c

IRI https://w3id.org/nuproc/ns#DefiniteOrganizationReference
Is Defined By NuProc Ontology — core
Description

  • An actor reference by which the source picked out one organization definitely: by its name, an abbreviation of it, or an office-holder standing for it. A group of organizations acting together as one party, such as a consortium, is one organization. Whether a recorded decision has identified which registered organization that is, is its resolution status (resolved or unresolved), not its kind.

  • 출처가 조직 하나를 확정적으로 가리킨 지칭 — 이름·약칭, 또는 그 조직을 대표하는 직위 보유자로. 함께 한 당사자로 움직이는 조직 무리 (컨소시엄 등) 도 조직 하나다. 어느 등록 조직인지를 기록된 결정이 식별했는지는 종류가 아니라 해소 상태(resolved 또는 unresolved)다.
Scope Note

  • These are the references that use a label whose registry mention_kind is organization or person_in_org_slot, and the references that use a candidate-set label whose article evidence pointed to one organization, or to the candidates acting together as one party, in the event-by-event resolution. In the design set, all of them are resolved. A reference that has a name but has not yet been matched also belongs here (rstat:unresolved) — it is not placed under UnnamedActorReference. A definite reference resolves to one organization (pattern: at most one resolvedTo). A reference that names a consortium resolves to the consortium organization, whose members are linked with org:hasMember

  • 레지스트리 mention_kind 가 organization · person_in_org_slot 인 표기를 쓴 지칭, 그리고 사건별 해소 (ADR-0040 §5 · ADR-0041 §3) 에서 기사 근거가 조직 하나, 또는 한 당사자로 함께 움직인 후보들을 가리킨 후보 집합 표기를 쓴 지칭이다. 설계 세트에서는 전부 resolved 다. 이름은 있으나 아직 대조되지 않은 지칭도 여기 속한다(rstat:unresolved) — UnnamedActorReference 로 두지 않는다. 확정 지칭은 조직 하나로 해소된다 (패턴 — resolvedTo 많아야 하나, ADR-0041 §3). 컨소시엄을 가리킨 지칭은 컨소시엄 조직으로 해소되고, 그 구성원은 org:hasMember 로 잇는다
Source

  • docs/adr/ADR-0031-reference-resolution-completed.md; entity resolution of the design set (organization labels: 34 kinds, 567 cases · person labels: 3 kinds, 3 cases)

  • ADR-0031; ENTITY_RESOLUTION_REPORT §5.1 (조직 표기 34종 567건 · 인물 표기 3종 3건)

National Side Reference c

IRI https://w3id.org/nuproc/ns#NationalSideReference
Is Defined By NuProc Ontology — core
Description

  • A reference denoting a national side rather than an organization, such as 'the US side'. Confusing it with organization aggregates would contaminate cross-case organization queries.

  • 조직이 아니라 국가 단위로 묶어 가리키는 지칭 (예: 미국 측). 조직 집계와 혼동하면 cross-case 조직 질의가 오염된다
Scope Note

  • Most national-side references in the design data come from labels of the form 'X (government)', which do not say which body of the government is meant, so they denote a national side rather than an organization (ADR-0023). Candidate-set labels whose article evidence pointed only to a national side in the event-by-event resolution also belong here. This is why state sponsorship rarely reaches the real-world layer: a national-side reference cannot be the bearer organization of a role

  • 설계 자료의 국가 측 지칭은 대부분 'X (government)' 꼴 표기에서 온다 — 정부의 어느 기관인지를 가리지 않으므로 조직이 아니라 국가 측이다 (ADR-0023). 사건별 해소 (ADR-0040 §5) 에서 기사 근거가 국가 쪽만 가리킨 후보 집합 표기도 여기 든다. 국가 후원 역할이 실세계 층에 드물게 오르는 까닭이다 — 국가 측 지칭은 역할의 담지 조직이 되지 못한다
